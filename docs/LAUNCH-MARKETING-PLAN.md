# Sadhai Coffee: 12-month launch and marketing plan
## Two outlets: Birtamode and Damak (Jhapa)

Prepared 27 Oct 2026. Month 1 = the month the first outlet opens. Every number marked **(assumption)** is a planning figure to replace with your real quote or measured data. Prices come from the live menu in `backend/scripts/seed.js`.

---

## 1. Where we start

**Product (already built):** order-ahead app and web, 90-second pickup, kiosk for counter tablets, eSewa / Fonepay / NepalPay QR, 10-cup pass (Rs 1,200, 30 days), free first cup then 3 cups at 30% off, referral (give a cup, get a cup), offer of the day, monthly limited drop, English and Nepali, station and manager dashboards with win-back lists.

**Menu and price ladder (VAT and service included):**

| Tier | Items | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Hot | Espresso, Americano, Cappuccino, Latte, Gulmi pour-over | Rs 100 |
| Flavoured | Mocha, Chiya latte, Gur cardamom latte | Rs 120 |
| Iced | Iced latte, Cold brew, Suntala cold brew | Rs 150 |
| Bites | Cardamom bun Rs 90, Butter croissant Rs 120 | Rs 90 to 120 |
| Merch / beans | Mug 450, Cap 550, Tote 650, Gulmi beans 250g 790 | Rs 450 to 790 |

**Brand voice:** warm, quick, a little dry. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "premium". Tagline: *Good coffee, every day* (सधैं राम्रो कफी). Keep this in every caption, poster and SMS.

**Honest read of the two markets (verify with a street walk in week 1):**

| | Birtamode | Damak |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Bigger commercial and trade hub, highway traffic, more cafes and competition | Compact town on the East-West Highway, schools and college, tea-garden economy, fewer specialty options |
| Likely customers | Traders, office and bank staff, college students, young families, highway travellers | Students and teachers, local professionals, tea-estate staff, shoppers |
| Strategy | **Flagship / brand outlet.** Win on speed, convenience, app and visibility | **Habit outlet.** Win on being first and on community, lower competition |
| Risk | Established cafes, price comparison | Smaller footfall, slower awareness, coffee still a new habit for many |

**Core insight:** at Rs 100 a hot coffee, Sadhai is priced at chiya-plus, not at café-premium. The job of marketing is to move people from "one chiya a day" to "one Sadhai a day", not to convince coffee people to switch. Build everything around *daily habit*, which is exactly what the 10-cup pass and the app are for.

## 3. Positioning and message

**One line:** Nepal's everyday specialty coffee, ready in 90 seconds, from hill-grown beans.

**Three proof points (use in rotation, never all at once):**
1. **Fast:** order ahead, pickup number waiting, 90 seconds.
2. **Fair:** Rs 100 a cup, price includes VAT and service, no surprises.
3. **Ours:** grown in Gulmi, roasted in Nepal, chiya-spiced and gur-cardamom drinks that taste like home.

**Local-language hooks (check with a native copywriter before printing):**
- "चिया भन्दा एक कदम माथि।" (One step above chiya.)
- "९० सेकेन्ड। एक कप। सधैं।" (90 seconds. One cup. Always.)
- "पहिलो कप हाम्रो तर्फबाट।" (Your first cup is on us.)
- "झापाको आफ्नै कफी।" (Jhapa's own coffee.)

**Never say:** premium, luxury, world-class, best coffee in Nepal. Say what is true: how fast, how much, where the beans are from.

## 5. Phased calendar

Nepali calendar anchors to plan around: Dashain and Tihar (Oct to Nov), Maghe Sankranti (mid-Jan), Valentine's Day (14 Feb), Holi (Mar), Nepali New Year / Baisakh 1 (mid-Apr), SEE and +2 exam seasons (Mar to Apr), summer heat (Apr to Jun), monsoon (Jun to Sep), Teej (Aug / Sep), school and college admission season (Baisakh to Jestha and Shrawan).

### Phase 0: Pre-launch (8 weeks before opening)

**Goal:** 1,000 sign-ups on the waitlist before a single cup is served.

| Week | Actions |
|---|---|
| -8 | Final site check at both locations (footfall count: 3 days × 3 time slots). Confirm brand colours, signage, uniforms, cups, sleeves with the cherry mark. Register Google Business Profile for both outlets (pin, hours, photos, category "Coffee shop"). |
| -7 | Create Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp Business. Announce "Coming to Birtamode and Damak" with the logo and a teaser of the Gulmi origin. Start a **waitlist page** on the website (name, phone, which outlet, student or not). |
| -6 | Hire and train team (see §11). Photograph the menu on the brand's illustrations. Record a 30-second founder video in Nepali. |
| -5 | **Naming-the-drink contest:** locals suggest a name for the Jhapa drink (e.g. a tea-and-coffee blend). Winner gets 30 free cups. Gets shares without paying. |
| -4 | Pre-launch tasting at three places: a bank branch, a college staff room, a cooperative. 40 to 60 people each. Collect phone numbers and reactions, film one quote per person with consent. |
| -3 | Start **Founding Member list**: first 500 sign-ups receive a Founding Member pass (10 cups for Rs 900 instead of Rs 1,200, **one-time, first 500 only**) and a numbered card. Scarcity is real and capped. |
| -2 | Soft opening for friends, family and staff (3 days, free). Fix the queue, the app, the kiosk and the QR payments. Do not skip this. |
| -1 | Local radio spots begin, banners go up, street-pole posters, distributor shops get stickers. Invite journalists, local FM hosts and 10 micro-influencers to a media morning. |

**Pre-launch budget (assumption):** Rs 150,000 to 200,000.

### Phase 1: Launch (Month 1, Birtamode first, Damak 3 to 5 weeks later)

**Why stagger:** one flagship gets all your attention, fixes the process, then Damak launches as a proven playbook with testimonials from Birtamode ("coming to Damak after Birtamode said yes").

**Birtamode launch week (Days 1 to 7):**
- **Day 1: Opening morning.** Ribbon cut by a respected local figure (a ward chair, a cooperative head, a school principal). Live local musician for 3 hours. First 100 customers get a free cup (this is the free-first-cup in the app, no extra cost to staff).
- **Day 1 to 7:** Free first cup in the app. Hot-seat challenge: order-and-pickup in under 90 seconds or the cup is free (put a visible timer on the counter). This is both a promise and content.
- **Day 3:** Student day. Show a college ID, get cardamom bun at Rs 50 with any coffee.
- **Day 5:** Shopkeepers and traders day: bulk order of 10 cups delivered to shops within 500 metres.
- **Day 7:** Open mic / music evening. Photographer on site.

**Birtamode first 30 days:**
- Hand-out sampling: 50 sample cups a day for two weeks at the bus park, banks, the campus gate, with a QR card for the free first cup.
- Door-to-door in office buildings: one box of 12 cold brews with a flyer and QR code.
- Partner shops carry "Sadhai pickup" stickers where customers can scan to order.
- Daily post: one "behind the bar" story, one customer, one menu item. Never only promos.

**Damak launch (3 to 5 weeks after Birtamode):**
- Same playbook, adjusted: more weight on **schools and college** and **tea-estate / agri community** tie-ins.
- Opening day: "Birtamode's coffee has arrived in Damak." Show Birtamode numbers ("2,000 cups in 4 weeks").
- Invite Birtamode's first 20 customers to come to the Damak opening as guests (cross-pollination, gives a story).

**Launch budget (assumption):** Rs 250,000 to 350,000 combined for both outlets (includes sampling, signage, opening events, paid media).

### Phase 2: Habit building (Months 2 to 4)

**Goal:** turn first-cup customers into daily regulars. This is the phase that decides the year.

| Lever | Action |
|---|---|
| **Pass push** | Every free-first-cup customer is shown the pass at pickup ("10 cups, Rs 1,200, any counter"). Staff script: "Your 4th cup this week would be free on a pass." Target 15% of active customers on a pass by Month 4. |
| **Referral** | Place "Give a cup. Get a cup." on every receipt, cup sleeve and the pickup screen. Monthly top referrer wins a Sadhai mug and 10 free cups. |
| **Win-back SMS** | Use the dashboard's win-back list: anyone who ordered 2 or more times but is silent for 14 days gets "Your usual is waiting. Latte, Rs 100." Test two messages. |
| **Offer of the day** | Different every day, predictable pattern: Mon Gur cardamom latte, Wed Mocha, Fri Cold brew combo, Sun Bun + Americano. Customers learn the rhythm. |
| **Morning rush loop** | 7 to 9 AM "Pickup before the bus": pre-order from home, pickup at counter. Partner with bus and ride operators and school drop-off lines. |
| **Loyalty tiers (soft)** | After 10, 25, 50 cups, send a thank-you and a surprise (free bun, free merch sticker). No new software needed. |
| **Review drive** | After every 5-star in-app rating, prompt for a Google Maps review with a direct link. Goal: 4.5 or higher and 80 reviews per outlet by Month 4. |

**Content rhythm (per outlet):** 4 Instagram posts, 7 stories, 2 Reels, 1 TikTok per week. One person owns this, 6 to 8 hours a week.

### Phase 3: Growth and community (Months 5 to 8)

**Goal:** become the place in each town, not just a shop.

- **Monthly drop** (limited drink): Month 5 Suntala cold brew (summer), Month 6 Mango cold foam (monsoon), Month 7 Roasted barley latte, Month 8 Ginger honey latte (monsoon chill). Announce 3 days early with a countdown.
- **Café events (one per outlet per month):** Poetry and open-mic, board games, sketching night, "Tea vs Coffee" taste-off, a first-time latte-art class for 10 people (Rs 300, includes a cup).
- **Campus ambassadors:** 3 paid student ambassadors per outlet (Rs 5,000 per month plus free coffee). They run campus giveaways, a student discount code, and collect feedback.
- **Corporate and cooperative plans:** office pass (20 cups per month, Rs 2,200, invoice monthly), coffee delivery to local offices at 10 AM, gift boxes for events.
- **Merch and beans:** Gulmi beans 250 g at Rs 790 as a gift item for Dashain/Tihar, sold with a mug. Photograph in homes, not shops.
- **Partnerships:** local bakeries (bun and croissant supply), ride-hail and rider groups, yoga studios, gyms, coaching centres, bookshops, co-working spaces. Cross-promote with QR cards.
- **Mid-year loyalty survey:** 5-question, 2 minutes, in-app. Reward 1 free cup. Use answers to adjust menu and hours.

### Phase 4: Festive and anniversary (Months 9 to 12)

- **Dashain / Tihar (Months 9 to 10 if opening Jan):** gift boxes (2 mugs + beans), "Tika" cup, Bhai Tika combo for siblings, family-pack of 6 cold brews, extended hours, gift pass sold as a physical card. Drive pass purchases as gifts: "Give 10 cups".
- **Winter (Months 11 to 12):** hot chocolate, chiya latte, gur cardamom promoted; "Warm before work" campaign; Maghe Sankranti special (til-laddu bun).
- **First anniversary:** "One year of Sadhai in Jhapa." Free cup for every customer who has ordered 20 or more times. Public thank-you with the real numbers ("X cups, Y farmers supported"). Plan the next outlet announcement.
- **Year-end review and Year 2 plan:** decide on a third outlet (Kakarbhitta, Bhadrapur, Surunga or Dhulabari) only if both outlets are cash-positive for 3 consecutive months.

## 6. Channel plan

### 6.1 Offline and local (60% of early effort)

| Channel | Role | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Brand and discovery | Daily story, 4 posts and 2 Reels a week. Content mix: 40% product, 30% people (customers, team), 20% place and community, 10% offers. |

| **Website / app** | Conversion | Fast landing page per outlet with the QR, opening hours, map, menu in Nepali and English. App store listing copy is in `store/STORE-LISTING.md`. |
| **Paid social** | Awareness and install | Meta ads geo-targeted 5 km around each outlet, ages 16 to 45. Objective: app install or waitlist sign-up. See §8. |
| **Influencers** | Social proof | 10 micro-influencers (3k to 20k followers) per outlet in the first 3 months. Pay in cups and a small fee (Rs 3,000 to 8,000) per post, with a clear brief and no scripted praise. |
| **Email** | Corporate and merch | Small list, monthly note. Optional. |

### 6.3 Using what the product already gives you

- **Free first cup** is the acquisition engine. Do not discount anything else in the first 90 days.
- **Dashboard win-back list** is the retention engine. Run it every Monday.
- **Offer of the day + monthly drop** are your content calendar. Plan the photo for each one in advance.
- **Kiosk** at the counter lets walk-ins register with consent (name and phone). Every walk-in should leave on your list.
- **Station comparison** in the manager dashboard shows which outlet is ahead. Share a friendly monthly scoreboard with both teams.

## 7. Offers and pricing rules (guardrails)

| Offer | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Free first cup | Once per phone number, app order only | Cost about Rs 70 to 90 per cup in ingredients and cup |
| Founding Member pass | 500 only, Rs 900 for 10 cups, before opening | Pre-sells, builds list |
| Student price | Show ID: bun at Rs 50 with any coffee, or Rs 90 Americano | Keep the main Rs 100 price unchanged |
| Referral | Both get a cup | Cap at 5 per month per customer |
| Office pass | 20 cups, Rs 2,200 | 10% under list for volume |
| Gift pass | Physical card, 10 cups | Dashain, Tihar, birthdays |
| Happy hour | 2 to 4 PM, cold brew Rs 120 | Fills the afternoon dip |
| Never | Permanent 50% off, "buy 1 get 1" on every item | Damages price perception |

**Margin check:** at Rs 100 per hot coffee, ingredient cost is roughly Rs 35 to 50 (coffee at Rs 2,500/kg, milk at Rs 110/L, cup, sugar, wastage). Keep promotions on items with the best margin (hot tier, bites) and use the costing screen to test every offer before launching it.

## 9. Measurement

**Weekly (every Monday, 30 minutes, both managers + owner):**
- Cups per outlet per day (vs target)
- New app users, first-cup redemptions, 14-day repeat rate
- Pass sales and active pass holders
- Average order value, share of orders via app / kiosk / counter
- Rating, number of new Google reviews, complaint themes
- Win-back SMS sent and returned
- Top 3 items, slowest 3 items

**Monthly:**
- Customer acquisition cost = marketing spend / new customers who returned at least once
- Cost per cup of promotions as % of revenue (target under 6% after Month 3)
- Social growth and best-performing posts
- Referral share of new customers (target 20% by Month 6)
- Outlet profit (see station breakeven in the dashboard)

**Decision rules:**
- If a channel's cost per returning customer is more than 3 times your best channel for 4 weeks, cut it.
- If 14-day repeat rate is below 35% at Month 3, stop acquisition spend and fix quality, speed and staff greetings first.
- If an outlet does less than 60% of its breakeven cups at Month 4, run a 2-week local push (sampling + school + office delivery) and re-evaluate.

## 11. Team and operations behind the marketing

Marketing fails if service fails. Before the first sales push:

- **Staff per outlet:** 1 manager, 2 baristas per shift, 1 floater. Train on the 90-second standard (practice with a timer), greeting in Nepali and English, how to pitch the pass in 10 seconds, how to ask for a review.
- **Marketing owner:** one person (can be part-time at start) responsible for content, WhatsApp, and Google profile. Plus a freelance photographer 1 day a month.
- **Cleanliness and look:** the first photo of the outlet on Google must be clean, bright and full of customers. Schedule a photo shoot at the busiest hour.
- **Stock discipline:** a stocked-out cold brew on a launch weekend is a lost customer. Use the central inventory and transfer screens to keep both outlets supplied; set reorder levels before launch.
- **Payment flow:** test eSewa, Fonepay and NepalPay QR in each outlet before opening; have a printed-QR fallback with staff confirmation (already built).
- **Complaint loop:** respond to any public complaint within 24 hours, offer a fix, then follow up.

## 12. Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Customers try once, never return | High | Pass push, win-back SMS, speed standard, fix top complaint every week |
| Competitor cuts price | Medium | Do not match; compete on speed, quality and loyalty |
| Slow start in Damak | Medium | Stagger launch, reuse Birtamode proof, heavier school/college presence, local ambassadors |
| Free cups abused | Medium | One per phone number, OTP-verified, cap referrals |
| Supply or power outage | Medium | Backup generator or inverter, 3-day stock buffer, cold-brew batches |
| Staff turnover | Medium | Train two people per role, written standards, small monthly incentive tied to ratings |
| Monsoon road closures reduce footfall | Medium | Order-ahead and delivery push in June to September |
| Over-spending on ads | Medium | Weekly spend vs returning customers review; hard monthly cap |
| Brand-voice drift | Low | Keep the do/don't list on the counter wall |
